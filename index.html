Why I switched from Arch Linux to Fedora Linux

Arch Linux was my first permanent Linux os, I used it because I kept hearing it on social media. I didn't install it normally, though, I used archinstall. The reason basically is because an update broke my out of tree driver, but I do always have otehr issues with Arch too.

What happened that ultimately pushed me away from Arch Linux

So, I ran checkupdates and saw that some linux firmware updates and some others are out, so of course I ran sudo pacman -Syu. When i rebooted my aic8800d80 usb wifi kept saying my wifi password was wrong. THat took me 2 days to fix and the most unexpected thing was the fix. First I tried downgrading, but that didn't work. Than I tried many things, but they were also in vain. The thing that fixed my wifi was running nmcli connection modify then something after it.
